==================================================

5. Recipient signs in or creates/uses their Firebase account.
6. Recipient explicitly accepts the invitation.
7. Only then are trusted claims/permissions applied.
8. Audit everything.

DO NOT silently grant staff privileges immediately.

Always require invitation acceptance.

- Pending Invitations
- Expired Invitations
- Revoked Staff

==================================================

Move or prominently expose ownership management inside:

- Owner enters target email.

- Recipient must authenticate and accept.
- Existing Owner remains Owner until acceptance completes atomically.
- Then claims/Staff records are updated atomically.
- Invalidate/refresh relevant tokens/claims.
- Audit both initiation and completion.
- Expire unused invitations.

Never transfer ownership just because an email was typed.

Cancel Transfer
Transfer Ownership
